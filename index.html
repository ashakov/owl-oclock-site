Telling time is harder than it looks

Two hands, two jobs, and the short one moves too. Most kids get stuck in the same few places, and most practice doesn't help them get unstuck.

"Which one is the hour hand again?"

Mixing up the hands is the classic mistake. A worksheet marks it wrong, but it can't explain the mix-up at the moment your child makes it.

Quiz apps just say "wrong"

A buzzer doesn't teach. Your child taps again, and learns to guess instead of read the clock.

Locked right after o'clock

Many clock apps open one level, then ask for a weekly subscription, or fill the screen with ads.

No time to plan lessons tonight

Owl O'Clock is built around one idea: every mistake gets a reason and a tip, one small step at a time.

How it works

Watch. Try. Get a tip.

Each of the seven levels follows the same calm loop. Ten minutes a day is plenty.

Watch

A short cartoon lesson shows one idea, like "the long hand points down for half past". It's read aloud by your device's voice.

Try

Your child sets the hands or picks the right time. Every round has 8 new questions, so answers can't simply be memorised.

Get a tip

When an answer is wrong, Tick explains the mix-up and gives a tip. After two tries, the answer is shown, so nobody gets stuck.

Questions parents ask

Before you download

Is it really free, or a trial?

Levels 1 to 3 (o'clock, half past and quarters), the Clock Lab, the daily review and the parent report are free, with no time limit. You only pay if you want levels 4 to 7.

What age is it for?

Children of about 5 to 8 who are learning to tell time at school or at home, roughly grades 1 to 3. It also suits homeschoolers and teachers who want a step-by-step unit.

Does one purchase cover all my children?

Yes. The full course gives up to 4 child profiles on the device, each with their own progress. On another phone or tablet signed in to the same Apple ID or Google account, tap Restore in the parent area.

Is it a subscription?

No. It's one payment of $4.99 in the US (your store shows your local price). Nothing renews and nothing is charged later.

Will my child see ads or prices?

No ads at all. The price only appears in the parent area, behind a parental gate.

Does it need the internet?

No. Lessons and practice work offline. You only need a connection to buy or restore the full course.

How is this different from a worksheet?

A worksheet is great for practice, and you can keep using it. The app adds what paper can't: the hands move, every round has new questions, and each wrong answer gets an explanation right away.

What data do you collect?

None. There's no account, no analytics and no advertising ID. Progress is stored on the device. See the privacy policy.
