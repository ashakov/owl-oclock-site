Privacy Policy

Owl O'Clock: Tell Time · Last updated 2 November 2026

Owl O'Clock is an app that teaches children to read a clock. It is made for children and their parents, and it is built to collect nothing.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party SDKs that collect data. We do not collect names, photos, contacts, location, device identifiers or usage statistics.

What stays on the device

The child's first name (optional), chosen avatar, stars and practice progress are saved only on the device, so the app can show progress to the child and the parent. This information never leaves the device and is not sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes it.

Voice

Lessons and questions are read aloud by the device's built-in text-to-speech voice, on the device. The app never uses the microphone or the camera.

Purchases

The one-time "full course" purchase is handled entirely by Apple's App Store or Google Play. We never see payment details. The app asks the store only whether this account owns the purchase.

Parental gate

Purchases, settings and links that leave the app are behind a parental gate (a question an adult answers).

Children's privacy

Because no personal information is collected, the app complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), GDPR-K and the app store rules for apps for children.
